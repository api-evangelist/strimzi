If you have visited the Strimzi website recently, you may have noticed something new: a small floating button in the bottom-right corner of every page. That is Kapa.ai , an AI-powered assistant now available across the entire Strimzi website, ready to help you find answers to your questions about Strimzi and Apache Kafka on Kubernetes. Why We Added an AI Assistant As Strimzi has grown, so has the volume of questions from the community.
